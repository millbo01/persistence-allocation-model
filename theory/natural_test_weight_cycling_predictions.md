# Natural test 12: weight cycling (predictions, committed before sources, 5 October 2026)

**Status:** theorising (phase 3); surface level. Weight cycling is deliberately **not** held out (James, 5 October 2026): it is where the model is most likely to be shaped further.

**Model version:** v0.9 (TIER_QUEUE_MODEL_v0.9.md).

**Source rule (James, 5 October 2026):** controlled studies only (randomised trials, controlled feeding or metabolic-ward studies, crossover trials, controlled animal experiments), with the same individuals measured repeatedly. Surveys, self-reported weight histories and observational cohorts are excluded, even if they support the model. CALERIE phase 2 is held out (H6) and is not used. The Minnesota Starvation Experiment is excluded.

**The system's clock (v0.9 rule):** durations are judged in units of one cycle (one restriction period plus its refeeding), fixed now.

**Mapping.** Currency: energy. The reserve: body fat. Working parts: lean tissue (muscle, organs). Load: energy demand above intake during restriction. Re-tuning can act on the reserve (its size and its place in the refill order), on demand (energy expenditure cut below what the smaller body needs, a demand-cutting change), and on intake.

**Contamination.** Known to Claude in outline before writing: that rats in early weight-cycling studies lost weight more slowly and regained faster in a second cycle (Brownell and colleagues, 1980s; also recalled by James); that energy expenditure falls after weight loss by more than the loss of mass predicts (metabolic-ward studies, Leibel and colleagues, 1990s); the title of the MATADOR trial (intermittent against continuous restriction). WC2, WC3 and WC6 are therefore not fresh tests.

| No. | Prediction | Model source |
|---|---|---|
| WC1 | After repeated cycles, the same animals end with a larger fat reserve than controls kept at stable intake, or than after a single cycle with the same total restriction, measured at the same time after the last refeeding | G15: frequency raises the reserve's size |
| WC2 | In later cycles, mass (or fat) is lost more slowly under the same restriction | Re-tuning to the threat met: demand cut and efficiency (G8, G9; RT1) |
| WC3 | In later cycles, fat is regained faster, and earlier relative to lean tissue | G15: frequency raises the reserve's place in the refill order |
| WC4 | Regain after restriction puts fat back before lean tissue, with fat overshooting its starting level, more so after longer (chronic) restriction than after short (acute) restriction | G10 |
| WC5 | Energy expenditure falls during restriction by more than the loss of mass predicts, and stays low for a time after refeeding; the effect fades over cycles of the system's clock rather than ending at refeeding | Demand-cutting re-tuning that fades (v0.9 memory) |
| WC6 | At equal total restriction, one deep restriction leaves a larger lasting fat reserve than several shallow ones, unless the shallow ones are frequent | G15 (engine, TQ8 C8; depends on arbitrary cap and memory) |
| WC7 | The effects of cycling fade once cycling stops, within a few cycle lengths, unless an episode was deeper than any before it | v0.9 memory, peak-referenced |

**Verdict rules.** Each prediction is marked consistent, partly consistent, inconsistent or not found, with sources and their design. Population-level or observational findings are not used (James's rule). A finding against the model is logged as found; any explanation must name the place first, then be checked.
