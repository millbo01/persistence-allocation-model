# Natural test 11: the Felicity ratio in muscle (predictions, committed before sources, 5 October 2026)

**Status:** theorising (phase 3); surface level. Muscle is not on the held-out list.

**Purpose.** Decide whether the Felicity ratio, held from the second review, goes into the model as a read-out of starting stage (TIER_QUEUE_MODEL_v0.7.md, open questions).

**The idea, translated (from the second review).** In materials, a reloaded structure gives no strain signal until the load passes the highest it has carried before (Kaiser effect). A damaged structure signals below that previous peak (Felicity effect). Felicity ratio = the load at which strain signals appear ÷ the previous peak load.

**What the model already implies.** After an acute, recovered episode, a part gains growth (a higher ceiling) or re-tuning (protection), so strain should appear only above the previous peak (ratio of 1 or more). After a scar, or before recovery is complete, the ceiling is lower, so strain should appear below the previous peak (ratio below 1). If this holds, the ratio is a read-out derived from existing rules (G7, G8), not a new mechanism.

**System.** Skeletal muscle under eccentric (lengthening) exercise. Strain signals: soreness, loss of maximal force, creatine kinase, swelling, range of motion. Load: eccentric work (number of contractions, intensity, muscle length).

**Contamination.** The repeated bout effect (a second bout of the same eccentric exercise causes less damage) is known to Claude in outline, and was touched in natural test 8 (muscle) and the re-tuning test (RT4: protection lasts 3 weeks to 6 months). FM1 is therefore not a fresh test. FM2 to FM5 are not known to Claude in detail.

| No. | Prediction | Model source |
|---|---|---|
| FM1 | After a recovered first bout, a second bout at the same load produces smaller strain signals (ratio of 1 or more at the previous peak) | Growth and re-tuning (G8) |
| FM2 | Protection is load-referenced: a second bout heavier than the first produces strain signals that grow with how far it exceeds the first, while bouts at or below the first are largely protected. A milder first bout protects against a heavier second one only partly | Kaiser effect translated; re-tuning to the threat met |
| FM3 | A second bout given before recovery is complete (a scar not yet resolved) produces strain signals at or below the previous peak load: ratio below 1, or no protection | Scar or incomplete recovery lowers the ceiling (G7) |
| FM4 | Protection fades with time without re-exposure, so the ratio drifts back towards 1 | Re-tuning fades (RT4) |
| FM5 | A compromised start (for example older muscle, or muscle with prior injury or disease) shows a smaller protective effect: a lower ratio | Starting state (G4); growth needs a renewable, coupled part |

**Verdict rules.** Each prediction is marked consistent, partly consistent, inconsistent or not found, with sources. If FM2 and FM3 hold, the Felicity ratio enters the model as a read-out of starting stage derived from G7 and G8. If FM2 fails (protection is not referenced to the previous peak load), the ratio is not adopted, because the load-referencing is what makes it a ratio.
